# Newsletter Style

Paste standing instructions here — tone, section emphasis, things to skip.
This file is read verbatim and appended to every Gemini prompt, so edits here
shape every future issue without touching code.

## Baseline (seeded 2026-07-23)

- Audience: business leaders, educators, and professionals learning to work with AI.
- Plain language, no unexplained jargon. Every item gets a paragraph summary
  and a shareable social post (≤280 chars, 2-3 hashtags).
- Mark rumours as rumours. Always include source links.
- Prefer practical takeaways over hype.
- Two-part format: Part 1 (News & Learning), Part 2 (Technical & Community).
