## 🎯 Prompt Engineering Tips

**Control Tokens for Generative Voice Synthesis**
Generative voice models allow you to embed specific control prompts directly within your text to manipulate delivery. For example, inserting marker tags like `[laughter]` or `[pause]` tells the model to synthesize a realistic chuckle or a natural breathing gap at that exact moment in the spoken audio.
**Key takeaway:** Use speech control prompts when building interactive voice assistants, training tools, or podcasts to inject emotion, pacing, and realistic human pauses into the output.
