## 🎯 Prompt Engineering Tips

**Dynamic Model-Routing Prompts**
Instead of asking one complex prompt to solve a massive, multi-step problem, write a "triage" system prompt that breaks the problem into sub-tasks and assigns them based on difficulty. For example, instruct an LLM to categorize steps as "simple text formatting" (which can be routed to a fast, cheap model) versus "deep logical reasoning" (which requires a premium model). This strategy ensures you only spend high-cost tokens on the hardest parts of your workflow, keeping your overall operations highly efficient.
**Key takeaway:** Use a master routing prompt to dissect complex projects into sub-tasks, ensuring you do not waste premium, expensive AI compute on basic formatting or simple calculations.
📱 Social post: Don't waste expensive AI tokens on simple tasks! Use a triage prompt to break complex projects down and route basic sub-tasks to faster, cheaper models, saving the heavy logic for premium LLMs. #PromptEngineering #AITips #AIOrchestration
[Source](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49026810)

**Session-Warming for Long-Context Chats**
When working with very long chat threads, large documents, or extensive codebases, AI models can suffer from slow response times (cold starts) because they must re-process massive amounts of historical context for every new message. You can keep your active session "warm" and highly responsive by using background tools to periodically trigger minor interactions with the assistant, keeping the server cache active. This simple trick prevents frustrating, multi-second delays when you resume your work.
**Key takeaway:** When working with massive documents or long-running threads, maintaining active background interactions keeps your session cached and your AI responses lightning-fast.
📱 Social post: Tired of waiting for Claude to re-read your massive documents in long chat sessions? Keeping your active sessions "warm" with background interactions prevents cold starts and keeps your AI responses lightning-fast. #PromptEngineering #AITips #Claude

**OneCLI**

OneCLI is an open-source credential gateway built specifically to prevent autonomous AI agents from leaking sensitive keys and passwords. AI agents often need credentials to interface with databases and external services, but they are vulnerable to prompt injection attacks that trick them into exposing these secrets. OneCLI resolves this by acting as a secure proxy: it strips raw API keys out of the agent's memory and environment, replacing them with placeholders, and securely injects the real credentials only at the network gateway layer.

**Key feature:** A secure network-level middle layer that keeps API keys and database credentials completely hidden from AI models and safe from prompt injection hacks.
